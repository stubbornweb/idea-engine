# Proposal format

[`ONE-SCREEN-FORMAT.md`](ONE-SCREEN-FORMAT.md) with one section added after Risk. Passes every check in [`WIN-WITHOUT-PITCHING.md`](WIN-WITHOUT-PITCHING.md).

The Risk section answers each anxiety from the intake.

```markdown
## Options

| | You get | Price | Ready |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | <smallest outcome that helps> | €<n> | <date> |
| **B: recommended** | <the outcome that fixes the pain> | €<n> | <date> |
| C | <B plus the next outcome> | €<n> | <date> |

<One line: why B.>

**Care plan:** €<n> a month after launch. <What it covers: upkeep, fixes, small changes per month.>

Reply with A, B or C and a start date that suits you.
```

## Care plan

Add the **Care plan** line when the product needs upkeep after launch: a live site, an app or an AI workflow. It is optional for the client, and it is not a fourth option.

## Diagnosis as option A

When `NOTES.md` holds two or more distinct struggling moments, option A is a **diagnosis**. The user can also ask for it in any proposal. B and C stay builds.

- **You get:** a ranked list of what to fix in their work, each item with its money math and a price range, and a demo of the top 2 items.
- **Price:** €750 for one interview, plus €200 for each extra person interviewed.
- **Credit:** add one line under the table. "The diagnosis fee comes off the first build invoice when you accept a build within 60 days."
